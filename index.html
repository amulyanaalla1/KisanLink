    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I get paid as a farmer?

    Payments are transferred directly to your linked UPI account within 24 hours of successful delivery.

    Who handles the delivery?

    We've partnered with local delivery services to ensure fast and reliable pickup from your farm and delivery to the customer.

    What if there is a problem with an order?

    You can raise a ticket through the app or use our 24/7 live chat. Our support team will resolve it immediately.
